The Good Hope feeling

Boards that belong to this coastline — Cape Town's beachbreaks, the Overberg's firing sandbanks, the wild pulls of the West Coast and trips to Mossel Bay.

The audience

Strong grom energy, balanced by experienced surfers looking for refined flow. Core traditional Cape Town surfer vibes with contemporary energy layered on top.

What we build

Style. Barrels. Vibes. Rail surfing. Trimming. Power surfing. Not performance-obsessed — soul-driven. Covering Thrusters, Fish, Mid-Length, and Longboards.
